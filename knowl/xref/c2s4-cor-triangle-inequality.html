Corollary 2.4.16 The triangle inequality in every dimension.

For \(\mathbf u,\mathbf v\in\mathbb R^n\text{,}\) \(\|\mathbf u+\mathbf v\|\le\|\mathbf u\|+\|\mathbf v\|\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗

Proof.
By Cauchy–Schwarz,
\begin{equation*} \|\mathbf u+\mathbf v\|^2=\|\mathbf u\|^2+2\mathbf u\cdot\mathbf v+\|\mathbf v\|^2\le(\|\mathbf u\|+\|\mathbf v\|)^2. \end{equation*}
Both sides are nonnegative, so taking square roots proves the claim promised in Section 1.5. Equality holds if one vector is zero or the two nonzero vectors point in the same direction.
