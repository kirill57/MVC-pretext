Theorem 19.2.2 Differentiating over a fixed region.

Let \(E\) be a fixed bounded closed integration region of the kind already used for double or triple integrals. Suppose a density \(\rho(X,t)\) and its time derivative are continuous on an open neighborhood of \(E\times[a,b]\text{.}\) For \(a<t<b\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} \frac{d}{dt}\iiint_E\rho(X,t)\,dV=\iiint_E\rho_t(X,t)\,dV. \end{equation*}
For a plane region the same statement uses double integrals and \(dA\text{.}\) The region must be fixed; a moving boundary contributes additional terms.
