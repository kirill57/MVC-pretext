Theorem 13.5.9 Short pieces of a path lie in balls.

Let \(\gamma:[a,b]\to D\) be continuous, with \(D\) open in Euclidean space. There is a number \(\delta>0\) such that the image of every nonempty closed subinterval of length less than \(\delta\) lies in an open ball contained in \(D\text{.}\) Thus a sufficiently fine finite subdivision has this property.
