Definition 2.7.6 Wedges of linear scalar measurements.

More generally, let \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) be linear functions from vectors to real numbers. Their wedge is the two-input measurement
\begin{equation*} (\alpha\wedge\beta)(\mathbf u,\mathbf v)=\alpha(\mathbf u)\beta(\mathbf v)-\alpha(\mathbf v)\beta(\mathbf u). \end{equation*}
Thus \(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) each take one vector, while \(\alpha\wedge\beta\) takes an ordered pair of vectors and returns a scalar. This definition does not define a wedge of two vectors.
