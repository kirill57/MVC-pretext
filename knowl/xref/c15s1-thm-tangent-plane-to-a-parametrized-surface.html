Theorem 15.1.10 Tangent plane to a parametrized surface.

Let
\begin{equation*} \mathbf r:D\to\mathbb R^3 \end{equation*}
be smooth on a neighborhood of an interior point \((u_0,v_0)\) and regular there. Set
\begin{equation*} \mathbf p=\mathbf r(u_0,v_0). \end{equation*}
Then the tangent plane to this local sheet at \(\mathbf p\) is
\begin{equation*} \boxed{ \mathbf X=\mathbf p+s\,\mathbf r_u(u_0,v_0)+t\,\mathbf r_v(u_0,v_0). } \end{equation*}
Equivalently, if
\begin{equation*} \mathbf n=\mathbf r_u(u_0,v_0)\times \mathbf r_v(u_0,v_0), \end{equation*}
then
\begin{equation*} \boxed{ \mathbf n\cdot(\mathbf X-\mathbf p)=0. } \end{equation*}
