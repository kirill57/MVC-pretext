Example 4.5.9 Latitude and longitude as grid curves.

The sphere of radius \(3\) can be parametrized by
\begin{equation*} \mathbf r(\theta,\phi) = (3\sin\phi\cos\theta,\, 3\sin\phi\sin\theta,\, 3\cos\phi), \end{equation*}
where
\begin{equation*} 0\le\theta\le2\pi,\qquad 0\le\phi\le\pi. \end{equation*}
Here \(\theta\) goes around the \(z\)-axis, and \(\phi\) measures angle down from the positive \(z\)-axis.
🔗
If \(0\lt\phi\lt\pi\) is fixed, then
\begin{equation*} z=3\cos\phi \end{equation*}
is fixed, and the point moves around a circle of latitude.
🔗
If \(\theta\) is fixed, then the point moves along a meridian from the north pole to the south pole.
