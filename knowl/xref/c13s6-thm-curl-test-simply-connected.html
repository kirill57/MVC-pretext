Theorem 13.6.16 Curl test on simply connected regions.

Let \(D\) be an open simply connected region.
🔗
In the plane, let
\begin{equation*} \omega=P\,dx+Q\,dy \end{equation*}
be a \(C^1\) \(1\)-form on \(D\text{.}\) If
\begin{equation*} P_y=Q_x \end{equation*}
throughout \(D\text{,}\) then \(\omega\) is exact on \(D\text{.}\)
🔗
In space, let
\begin{equation*} \mathbf F=(P,Q,R) \end{equation*}
be a \(C^1\) vector field on \(D\text{.}\) If
\begin{equation*} \nabla\times\mathbf F=\mathbf 0 \end{equation*}
throughout \(D\text{,}\) then \(\mathbf F\) is conservative on \(D\text{.}\)
