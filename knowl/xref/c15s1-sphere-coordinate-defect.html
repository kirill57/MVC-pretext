Failure of regularity alone does not settle the geometry. The spherical coordinates of Example 4.5.9 collapse at a pole, but near the north pole the same sphere has the regular graph patch
\begin{equation*} \mathbf q(u,v)=(u,v,\sqrt{9-u^2-v^2}),\qquad u^2+v^2\lt9. \end{equation*}
At \((0,0)\) its coordinate tangent vectors are \((1,0,0)\) and \((0,1,0)\text{.}\) The sphere therefore has a tangent plane there. The cone’s three independent generator directions, rather than merely a zero cross product, rule out a plane at its tip.
