Definition 3.4.17 Determinants in any finite dimension.

For an \(n\times n\) matrix \(M=(m_{ij})\text{,}\) a permutation \(\sigma\) is a rearrangement \((\sigma(1),\ldots,\sigma(n))\) of \((1,\ldots,n)\text{.}\) Its sign is \(\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)=(-1)^N\text{,}\) where \(N\) counts the pairs \(i<j\) with \(\sigma(i)>\sigma(j)\text{.}\) Define
\begin{equation*} \det M=\sum_{\sigma}\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\,m_{\sigma(1),1}\cdots m_{\sigma(n),n}, \end{equation*}
summing over every permutation. This is the earlier formula when \(n=2\) or \(n=3\text{;}\) for \(n=1\) it is the single matrix entry.
