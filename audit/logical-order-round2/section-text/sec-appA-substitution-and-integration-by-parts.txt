[5-5 title sec-appA-substitution-and-integration-by-parts default_required]
Substitution and integration by parts

[8-8 title subsec-appA-subs-substitution default_required]
Substitution

[10-10 p subsec-appA-subs-substitution default_required]
Substitution reverses the chain rule.

[12-20 p subsec-appA-subs-substitution default_required]
If
      u=g(x),
      then
      \boxed{du=g'(x)\,dx.}
      \boxed{\int f(g(x))g'(x)\,dx=\int f(u)\,du.}
      For definite integrals,
      \boxed{\int_a^b f(g(x))g'(x)\,dx=\int_{g(a)}^{g(b)} f(u)\,du.}

[24-24 title subsec-appA-subs-substitution-checklist default_required]
Substitution checklist

[26-34 p subsec-appA-subs-substitution-checklist default_required]
1.\amp \text{Choose }u=g(x).
        2.\amp \text{Compute }du=g'(x)\,dx.
        3.\amp \text{Replace the integrand and differential.}
        4.\amp \text{For definite integrals, change the limits.}
        5.\amp \text{For indefinite integrals, substitute back to }x.

[38-38 title subsec-appA-subs-common-substitution-patterns default_required]
Common substitution patterns

[40-45 p subsec-appA-subs-common-substitution-patterns default_required]
\int f(ax+b)\,dx\quad\text{use}\quad u=ax+b.
      \int f(x^2)\,2x\,dx\quad\text{use}\quad u=x^2.
      \int f(\sin x)\cos x\,dx\quad\text{use}\quad u=\sin x.
      \int f(\cos x)(-\sin x)\,dx\quad\text{use}\quad u=\cos x.

[49-49 title subsec-appA-subs-warning-substitution default_required]
Warning

[51-56 p subsec-appA-subs-warning-substitution default_required]
The substitution
      u=g(x)
      requires the matching factor
      g'(x)\,dx.

[58-64 p subsec-appA-subs-warning-substitution default_required]
For example,
      \int 2x e^{x^2}\,dx
      is directly handled by u=x^2, but
      \int e^{x^2}\,dx
      is not.

[68-68 title subsec-appA-subs-integration-by-parts default_required]
Integration by parts

[70-70 p subsec-appA-subs-integration-by-parts default_required]
Integration by parts reverses the product rule.

[72-76 p subsec-appA-subs-integration-by-parts default_required]
\boxed{\int u\,dv=uv-\int v\,du.}
      For definite integrals,
      \boxed{\int_a^b u\,dv=\left[uv\right]_a^b-\int_a^b v\,du.}

[80-80 title subsec-appA-subs-useful-choices default_required]
Useful choices

[82-87 p subsec-appA-subs-useful-choices default_required]
For
      \int x e^x\,dx,
      take
      u=x,\qquad dv=e^x\,dx.

[89-94 p subsec-appA-subs-useful-choices default_required]
For
      \int x\cos x\,dx,
      take
      u=x,\qquad dv=\cos x\,dx.

[96-101 p subsec-appA-subs-useful-choices default_required]
For
      \int \ln x\,dx,
      take
      u=\ln x,\qquad dv=dx.

[105-105 title subsec-appA-subs-warning-integration-by-parts default_required]
Warning

[107-111 p subsec-appA-subs-warning-integration-by-parts default_required]
Integration by parts creates a boundary term:
      [uv]_a^b.
      Do not drop it.
