[5-5 title sec-appB-euler-s-formula default_required]
Euler's formula

[8-8 title appB-euler-euler-form default_required]
Euler form

[10-14 p appB-euler-euler-form default_required]
\boxed{e^{i\theta}=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta.}
      Therefore
      \boxed{|e^{i\theta}|=1.}

[18-18 title appB-euler-polar-form-with-euler-notation default_required]
Polar form with Euler notation

[20-24 p appB-euler-polar-form-with-euler-notation default_required]
\boxed{r(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)=re^{i\theta}.}
      So every nonzero complex number can be written as
      \boxed{z=re^{i\theta}.}

[28-28 title appB-euler-multiplication default_required]
Multiplication

[30-32 p appB-euler-multiplication default_required]
\boxed{(re^{i\alpha})(se^{i\beta})=rs e^{i(\alpha+\beta)}.}

[36-36 title appB-euler-powers default_required]
Powers

[38-40 p appB-euler-powers default_required]
\boxed{(re^{i\theta})^n=r^n e^{in\theta}.}

[44-44 title appB-euler-rotation default_required]
Rotation

[46-51 p appB-euler-rotation default_required]
Multiplying by
      e^{i\theta}
      rotates the plane by angle \theta counterclockwise and keeps lengths unchanged.
      \boxed{z\mapsto e^{i\theta}z}

[55-55 title appB-euler-special-values default_required]
Special values

[57-63 p appB-euler-special-values default_required]
\boxed{e^{i0}=1}
      \boxed{e^{i\pi/2}=i}
      \boxed{e^{i\pi}=-1}
      \boxed{e^{2\pi i}=1}
      \boxed{e^{i\pi}+1=0.}

[67-67 title appB-euler-sine-and-cosine-from-exponentials default_required]
Sine and cosine from exponentials

[69-72 p appB-euler-sine-and-cosine-from-exponentials default_required]
\boxed{\cos\theta=\frac{e^{i\theta}+e^{-i\theta}}{2}}
      \boxed{\sin\theta=\frac{e^{i\theta}-e^{-i\theta}}{2i}}

[76-76 title appB-euler-euler-formula-and-trig-identities default_required]
Euler formula and trig identities

[78-84 p appB-euler-euler-formula-and-trig-identities default_required]
The identity
      e^{i(\alpha+\beta)}=e^{i\alpha}e^{i\beta}
      contains the angle addition formulas:
      \cos(\alpha+\beta)=\cos\alpha\cos\beta-\sin\alpha\sin\beta,
      \sin(\alpha+\beta)=\sin\alpha\cos\beta+\cos\alpha\sin\beta.

[88-88 title appB-euler-common-warning default_required]
Common warning

[89-94 p appB-euler-common-warning default_required]
The symbol
      e^{i\theta}
      does not mean growth away from the origin. It lies on the unit circle. It represents
      rotation.
