[5-5 title sec-appD-grad-curl-and-divergence-in-common-coordinates default_required]
Grad, curl, and divergence in common coordinates

[8-8 title appD-vecop-cartesian-coordinates default_required]
Cartesian coordinates

[10-14 p appD-vecop-cartesian-coordinates default_required]
For
      f=f(x,y,z),
      \boxed{\nabla f=f_x\,\mathbf i+f_y\,\mathbf j+f_z\,\mathbf k.}

[16-20 p appD-vecop-cartesian-coordinates default_required]
For
      \mathbf F=(P,Q,R),
      \boxed{\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=P_x+Q_y+R_z.}

[22-34 p appD-vecop-cartesian-coordinates default_required]
\boxed{
        \nabla\times\mathbf F
        =
        \begin{vmatrix}
        \mathbf i\amp\mathbf j\amp\mathbf k\\
        \partial_x\amp\partial_y\amp\partial_z\\
        P\amp Q\amp R
        \end{vmatrix}.
        }

[36-39 p appD-vecop-cartesian-coordinates default_required]
That is,
      \boxed{\nabla\times\mathbf F=(R_y-Q_z)\mathbf i+(P_z-R_x)\mathbf j+(Q_x-P_y)\mathbf k.}

[41-44 p appD-vecop-cartesian-coordinates default_required]
The Laplacian is
      \boxed{\Delta f=\nabla\cdot\nabla f=f_{xx}+f_{yy}+f_{zz}.}

[48-48 title appD-vecop-plane-cartesian-formulas default_required]
Plane Cartesian formulas

[50-55 p appD-vecop-plane-cartesian-formulas default_required]
For
      \mathbf F=(P,Q),
      the scalar curl in the plane is
      \boxed{\operatorname{curl}_{\mathrm{plane}}\mathbf F=Q_x-P_y.}

[57-60 p appD-vecop-plane-cartesian-formulas default_required]
The associated exterior derivative is
      \boxed{d(P\,dx+Q\,dy)=(Q_x-P_y)\,dx\wedge dy.}

[64-64 title appD-vecop-polar-coordinates default_required]
Polar coordinates

[66-69 p appD-vecop-polar-coordinates default_required]
Write
      \mathbf F=F_r\,\widehat{\mathbf e}_r+F_\theta\,\widehat{\mathbf e}_\theta.

[71-74 p appD-vecop-polar-coordinates default_required]
Gradient:
      \boxed{\nabla f=f_r\,\widehat{\mathbf e}_r+\frac1r f_\theta\,\widehat{\mathbf e}_\theta.}

[76-79 p appD-vecop-polar-coordinates default_required]
Divergence:
      \boxed{\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=\frac1r\frac{\partial}{\partial r}(rF_r)+\frac1r\frac{\partial F_\theta}{\partial\theta}.}

[81-84 p appD-vecop-polar-coordinates default_required]
Scalar curl:
      \boxed{\operatorname{curl}_{\mathrm{plane}}\mathbf F=\frac1r\frac{\partial}{\partial r}(rF_\theta)-\frac1r\frac{\partial F_r}{\partial\theta}.}

[86-89 p appD-vecop-polar-coordinates default_required]
Laplacian:
      \boxed{\Delta f=f_{rr}+\frac1r f_r+\frac1{r^2}f_{\theta\theta}.}

[93-93 title appD-vecop-cylindrical-coordinates default_required]
Cylindrical coordinates

[95-98 p appD-vecop-cylindrical-coordinates default_required]
Write
      \mathbf F=F_r\,\widehat{\mathbf e}_r+F_\theta\,\widehat{\mathbf e}_\theta+F_z\,\widehat{\mathbf e}_z.

[100-103 p appD-vecop-cylindrical-coordinates default_required]
Gradient:
      \boxed{\nabla f=f_r\,\widehat{\mathbf e}_r+\frac1r f_\theta\,\widehat{\mathbf e}_\theta+f_z\,\widehat{\mathbf e}_z.}

[105-108 p appD-vecop-cylindrical-coordinates default_required]
Divergence:
      \boxed{\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=\frac1r\frac{\partial}{\partial r}(rF_r)+\frac1r\frac{\partial F_\theta}{\partial\theta}+\frac{\partial F_z}{\partial z}.}

[110-139 p appD-vecop-cylindrical-coordinates default_required]
Curl:
      
        \boxed{
        \begin{aligned}
        \nabla\times\mathbf F
        \amp=
        \frac1r
        \left(
            \frac{\partial F_z}{\partial\theta}
            -
            \frac{\partial}{\partial z}(rF_\theta)
        \right)\widehat{\mathbf e}_r\\[1mm]
        \amp\quad+
        \left(
            \frac{\partial F_r}{\partial z}
            -
            \frac{\partial F_z}{\partial r}
        \right)\widehat{\mathbf e}_\theta\\[1mm]
        \amp\quad+
        \frac1r
        \left(
            \frac{\partial}{\partial r}(rF_\theta)
            -
            \frac{\partial F_r}{\partial\theta}
        \right)\widehat{\mathbf e}_z.
        \end{aligned}
        }

[141-144 p appD-vecop-cylindrical-coordinates default_required]
Laplacian:
      \boxed{\Delta f=f_{rr}+\frac1r f_r+\frac1{r^2}f_{\theta\theta}+f_{zz}.}

[148-148 title appD-vecop-spherical-coordinates default_required]
Spherical coordinates

[150-153 p appD-vecop-spherical-coordinates default_required]
Write
      \mathbf F=F_\rho\,\widehat{\mathbf e}_\rho+F_\phi\,\widehat{\mathbf e}_\phi+F_\theta\,\widehat{\mathbf e}_\theta.

[155-158 p appD-vecop-spherical-coordinates default_required]
Gradient:
      \boxed{\nabla f=f_\rho\,\widehat{\mathbf e}_\rho+\frac1\rho f_\phi\,\widehat{\mathbf e}_\phi+\frac1{\rho\sin\phi}f_\theta\,\widehat{\mathbf e}_\theta.}

[160-163 p appD-vecop-spherical-coordinates default_required]
Divergence:
      \boxed{\nabla\cdot\mathbf F=\frac1{\rho^2}\frac{\partial}{\partial\rho}(\rho^2F_\rho)+\frac1{\rho\sin\phi}\frac{\partial}{\partial\phi}(\sin\phi\,F_\phi)+\frac1{\rho\sin\phi}\frac{\partial F_\theta}{\partial\theta}.}

[165-198 p appD-vecop-spherical-coordinates default_required]
Curl:
      
        \boxed{
        \begin{aligned}
        \nabla\times\mathbf F
        \amp=
        \frac1{\rho\sin\phi}
        \left(
            \frac{\partial}{\partial\phi}(\sin\phi\,F_\theta)
            -
            \frac{\partial F_\phi}{\partial\theta}
        \right)
        \widehat{\mathbf e}_\rho\\[1mm]
        \amp\quad+
        \frac1\rho
        \left(
            \frac1{\sin\phi}\frac{\partial F_\rho}{\partial\theta}
            -
            \frac{\partial}{\partial\rho}(\rho F_\theta)
        \right)
        \widehat{\mathbf e}_\phi\\[1mm]
        \amp\quad+
        \frac1\rho
        \left(
            \frac{\partial}{\partial\rho}(\rho F_\phi)
            -
            \frac{\partial F_\rho}{\partial\phi}
        \right)
        \widehat{\mathbf e}_\theta.
        \end{aligned}
        }

[200-203 p appD-vecop-spherical-coordinates default_required]
Laplacian:
      \boxed{\Delta f=f_{\rho\rho}+\frac2\rho f_\rho+\frac1{\rho^2}f_{\phi\phi}+\frac{\cot\phi}{\rho^2}f_\phi+\frac1{\rho^2\sin^2\phi}f_{\theta\theta}.}

[205-208 p appD-vecop-spherical-coordinates default_required]
Equivalently,
      \boxed{\Delta f=\frac1{\rho^2}\frac{\partial}{\partial\rho}\left(\rho^2 f_\rho\right)+\frac1{\rho^2\sin\phi}\frac{\partial}{\partial\phi}\left(\sin\phi\,f_\phi\right)+\frac1{\rho^2\sin^2\phi}f_{\theta\theta}.}

[212-212 title appD-vecop-component-warning default_required]
Component warning

[215-219 p appD-vecop-warning-components default_required]
In polar, cylindrical, and spherical formulas,
        F_r,\ F_\theta,\ F_\rho,\ F_\phi,\ F_z
        are components in the local unit-vector basis, not Cartesian components.

[221-227 p appD-vecop-warning-components default_required]
For example,
        \mathbf F=F_r\widehat{\mathbf e}_r+F_\theta\widehat{\mathbf e}_\theta
        does not mean
        \mathbf F=(F_r,F_\theta)
        in Cartesian coordinates.

[232-232 title appD-vecop-singularity-warning default_required]
Singularity warning

[235-238 p appD-vecop-warning-singularities default_required]
Polar and cylindrical formulas with 1/r require
        r\gt 0.

[240-243 p appD-vecop-warning-singularities default_required]
Spherical formulas with 1/\rho require
        \rho\gt 0.

[245-248 p appD-vecop-warning-singularities default_required]
Spherical formulas with 1/\sin\phi require
        \sin\phi\ne0.

[250-253 p appD-vecop-warning-singularities default_required]
Coordinate singularities do not always mean the field itself is singular. They may mean
        the coordinate system has broken down.
