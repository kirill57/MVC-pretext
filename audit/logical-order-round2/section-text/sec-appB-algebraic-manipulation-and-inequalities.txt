[8-8 title appB-alg-factoring-patterns default_required]
Factoring patterns

[10-12 p appB-alg-factoring-patterns default_required]
\boxed{a^2-b^2=(a-b)(a+b)}

[14-16 p appB-alg-factoring-patterns default_required]
\boxed{a^2+2ab+b^2=(a+b)^2}

[18-20 p appB-alg-factoring-patterns default_required]
\boxed{a^2-2ab+b^2=(a-b)^2}

[22-24 p appB-alg-factoring-patterns default_required]

[26-28 p appB-alg-factoring-patterns default_required]

[30-32 p appB-alg-factoring-patterns default_required]
\boxed{x^2+(p+q)x+pq=(x+p)(x+q)}

[36-36 title appB-alg-quadratic-formula default_required]
Quadratic formula

[38-43 p appB-alg-quadratic-formula default_required]
If
      ax^2+bx+c=0, \qquad a\ne0,
      then
      \boxed{x=\frac{-b\pm\sqrt{b^2-4ac}}{2a}.}

[45-48 p appB-alg-quadratic-formula default_required]
The discriminant is
      \boxed{\Delta=b^2-4ac.}

[51-51 title appB-alg-tab-discriminant default_required]
Sign of the discriminant.

[53-53 cell appB-alg-tab-discriminant default_required]
\Delta\gt0

[53-53 cell appB-alg-tab-discriminant default_required]
two distinct real roots

[54-54 cell appB-alg-tab-discriminant default_required]
\Delta=0

[54-54 cell appB-alg-tab-discriminant default_required]
one repeated real root

[55-55 cell appB-alg-tab-discriminant default_required]
\Delta\lt0

[55-55 cell appB-alg-tab-discriminant default_required]
two complex conjugate roots

[61-61 title appB-alg-completing-the-square default_required]
Completing the square

[63-65 p appB-alg-completing-the-square default_required]
\boxed{x^2+bx=\left(x+\frac b2\right)^2-\left(\frac b2\right)^2.}

[67-71 p appB-alg-completing-the-square default_required]
More generally,
      \boxed{ax^2+bx+c=a\left(x+\frac{b}{2a}\right)^2+c-\frac{b^2}{4a}}
      for a\ne0.

[93-93 title appB-alg-distance-on-the-line default_required]
Distance on the line

[95-98 p appB-alg-distance-on-the-line default_required]
\boxed{|x-a|}
      is the distance from x to a.

[100-102 p appB-alg-distance-on-the-line default_required]
\boxed{|x-a|\lt r \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad a-r\lt x\lt a+r.}

[104-106 p appB-alg-distance-on-the-line default_required]
\boxed{|x-a|\le r \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad a-r\le x\le a+r.}

[108-110 p appB-alg-distance-on-the-line default_required]
\boxed{|x-a|\gt r \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad x\lt a-r \text{ or } x\gt a+r.}

[114-114 title appB-alg-absolute-value-rules default_required]
Absolute value rules

[116-118 p appB-alg-absolute-value-rules default_required]
\boxed{|ab|=|a|\,|b|}

[120-122 p appB-alg-absolute-value-rules default_required]
\boxed{\left|\frac ab\right|=\frac{|a|}{|b|} \qquad (b\ne0)}

[124-127 p appB-alg-absolute-value-rules default_required]
\boxed{|a+b|\le |a|+|b|}
      This is the triangle inequality.

[129-131 p appB-alg-absolute-value-rules default_required]
\boxed{\bigl||a|-|b|\bigr|\le |a-b|.}

[135-135 title appB-alg-inequality-rules default_required]
Inequality rules

[137-140 p appB-alg-inequality-rules default_required]
Adding or subtracting the same number preserves the inequality:
      a\lt b \quad\Longrightarrow\quad a+c\lt b+c.

[142-145 p appB-alg-inequality-rules default_required]
Multiplying by a positive number preserves the inequality:
      a\lt b,\ c\gt0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad ac\lt bc.

[147-150 p appB-alg-inequality-rules default_required]
Multiplying by a negative number reverses the inequality:
      a\lt b,\ c\lt0 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad ac\gt bc.

[154-154 title appB-alg-quadratic-inequalities default_required]
Quadratic inequalities

[156-162 p appB-alg-quadratic-inequalities default_required]
To solve
      ax^2+bx+c\gt0
      or
      ax^2+bx+c\lt0,
      use this process:

[165-165 li appB-alg-quadratic-inequalities default_required]
Find the zeros.

[166-166 li appB-alg-quadratic-inequalities default_required]
Put the zeros on the number line.

[167-167 li appB-alg-quadratic-inequalities default_required]
Test one point in each interval.

[168-168 li appB-alg-quadratic-inequalities default_required]
Include endpoints only for \le or \ge.

[173-173 title appB-alg-exponent-rules default_required]
Exponent rules

[175-177 p appB-alg-exponent-rules default_required]
\boxed{a^m a^n=a^{m+n}}

[179-181 p appB-alg-exponent-rules default_required]
\boxed{\frac{a^m}{a^n}=a^{m-n} \qquad (a\ne0)}

[183-185 p appB-alg-exponent-rules default_required]
\boxed{(a^m)^n=a^{mn}}

[187-189 p appB-alg-exponent-rules default_required]
\boxed{(ab)^n=a^n b^n}

[191-193 p appB-alg-exponent-rules default_required]
\boxed{a^{-n}=\frac1{a^n} \qquad (a\ne0)}

[195-197 p appB-alg-exponent-rules default_required]
\boxed{a^{1/n}=\sqrt[n]{a}}

[201-201 title appB-alg-logarithm-rules default_required]
Logarithm rules

[203-205 p appB-alg-logarithm-rules default_required]
For a\gt0, b\gt0, and r\in\mathbb R,

[207-209 p appB-alg-logarithm-rules default_required]
\boxed{\ln(ab)=\ln a+\ln b}

[211-213 p appB-alg-logarithm-rules default_required]
\boxed{\ln\left(\frac ab\right)=\ln a-\ln b}

[215-217 p appB-alg-logarithm-rules default_required]
\boxed{\ln(a^r)=r\ln a}

[219-221 p appB-alg-logarithm-rules default_required]
\boxed{e^{\ln a}=a}

[223-225 p appB-alg-logarithm-rules default_required]
\boxed{\ln(e^x)=x}

[229-229 title appB-alg-common-warnings default_required]
Common warnings

[232-232 p appB-alg-common-warnings default_required]
Do not divide by a quantity unless it is known to be nonzero.

[234-234 p appB-alg-common-warnings default_required]
Do not take square roots of both sides of an inequality without tracking signs.

[236-241 p appB-alg-common-warnings default_required]
x^2\gt9
        means
        x\lt-3 \quad\text{or}\quad x\gt3,
        not just x\gt3.

[243-247 p appB-alg-common-warnings default_required]
The identity
        \sqrt{x^2}=|x|
        is usually safer than writing \sqrt{x^2}=x.
