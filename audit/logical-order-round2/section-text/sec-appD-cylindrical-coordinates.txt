[5-5 title sec-appD-cylindrical-coordinates default_required]
Cylindrical coordinates

[8-8 title appD-cyl-conversion-formulas default_required]
Conversion formulas

[10-14 p appD-cyl-conversion-formulas default_required]
Cylindrical coordinates are polar coordinates in the xy-plane plus height
      z.
      \boxed{(r,\theta,z).}

[16-19 p appD-cyl-conversion-formulas default_required]
Conversion to Cartesian:
      \boxed{x=r\cos\theta, \qquad y=r\sin\theta, \qquad z=z.}

[21-24 p appD-cyl-conversion-formulas default_required]
Conversion from Cartesian:
      \boxed{r^2=x^2+y^2, \qquad \tan\theta=\frac yx, \qquad z=z.}

[28-28 title appD-cyl-usual-ranges default_required]
Usual ranges

[30-32 p appD-cyl-usual-ranges default_required]
\boxed{r\ge0, \qquad 0\le\theta\lt2\pi, \qquad -\infty\lt z\lt\infty.}

[36-36 title appD-cyl-unit-vectors default_required]
Unit vectors

[38-42 p appD-cyl-unit-vectors default_required]
\boxed{\widehat{\mathbf e}_r=(\cos\theta,\sin\theta,0)}
      \boxed{\widehat{\mathbf e}_\theta=(-\sin\theta,\cos\theta,0)}
      \boxed{\widehat{\mathbf e}_z=(0,0,1).}

[46-46 title appD-cyl-small-displacement default_required]
Small displacement

[48-50 p appD-cyl-small-displacement default_required]
\boxed{d\mathbf r = \widehat{\mathbf e}_r\,dr + \widehat{\mathbf e}_\theta\,r\,d\theta + \widehat{\mathbf e}_z\,dz.}

[54-54 title appD-cyl-line-element default_required]
Line element

[56-58 p appD-cyl-line-element default_required]
\boxed{ds^2=dr^2+r^2\,d\theta^2+dz^2.}

[62-62 title appD-cyl-volume-element default_required]
Volume element

[64-67 p appD-cyl-volume-element default_required]
Ordinary volume:
      \boxed{dV=r\,dr\,d\theta\,dz.}

[69-72 p appD-cyl-volume-element default_required]
Oriented volume:
      \boxed{dx\wedge dy\wedge dz = r\,dr\wedge d\theta\wedge dz.}

[76-76 title appD-cyl-coordinate-surfaces default_required]
Coordinate surfaces

[78-81 p appD-cyl-coordinate-surfaces default_required]
Cylinder:
      \boxed{r=a.}

[83-86 p appD-cyl-coordinate-surfaces default_required]
Vertical half-plane:
      \boxed{\theta=\theta_0.}

[88-91 p appD-cyl-coordinate-surfaces default_required]
Horizontal plane:
      \boxed{z=c.}

[95-95 title appD-cyl-useful-equations default_required]
Useful equations

[97-100 p appD-cyl-useful-equations default_required]
Cylinder of radius a:
      \boxed{x^2+y^2=a^2 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad r=a.}

[102-105 p appD-cyl-useful-equations default_required]
Paraboloid:
      \boxed{z=x^2+y^2 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad z=r^2.}

[107-110 p appD-cyl-useful-equations default_required]
Cone:
      \boxed{z^2=x^2+y^2 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad z^2=r^2.}

[114-114 title appD-cyl-cylindrical-integration default_required]
Cylindrical integration

[116-118 p appD-cyl-cylindrical-integration default_required]
\boxed{\iiint_E f(x,y,z)\,dV = \iiint_{\widetilde E} f(r\cos\theta,r\sin\theta,z)\, r\,dr\,d\theta\,dz.}

[122-122 title appD-cyl-common-warnings default_required]
Common warnings

[125-128 p appD-cyl-common-warnings default_required]
Cylindrical r is distance from the z-axis, not distance from the origin
        in space.

[130-134 p appD-cyl-common-warnings default_required]
The factor r in
        dV=r\,dr\,d\theta\,dz
        must not be dropped.

[136-138 p appD-cyl-common-warnings default_required]
At r=0, the angle \theta is not unique.
