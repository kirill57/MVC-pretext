[5-5 title sec-appE-pullbacks default_required]
Pullbacks

[8-8 title appE-pull-meaning default_required]
Meaning

[10-10 p appE-pull-meaning default_required]
A pullback rewrites a form in new parameters.

[12-18 p appE-pull-meaning default_required]
If
      F:\text{parameter space}\to\text{physical space},
      then
      \boxed{F^*\omega}
      is the form \omega written in the parameter variables.

[20-20 p appE-pull-meaning default_required]
Pullbacks are the form version of substitution.

[24-24 title appE-pull-along-a-curve default_required]
Pullback along a curve

[26-33 p appE-pull-along-a-curve default_required]
Let
      \mathbf r(t)=(x(t),y(t),z(t)).
      Then
      \boxed{\mathbf r^*(dx)=x'(t)\,dt,}
      \boxed{\mathbf r^*(dy)=y'(t)\,dt,}
      \boxed{\mathbf r^*(dz)=z'(t)\,dt.}

[35-51 p appE-pull-along-a-curve default_required]
For
      \omega=P\,dx+Q\,dy+R\,dz,
      
        \boxed{
        \begin{aligned}
        \mathbf r^*\omega
        \amp=
        \bigl(P(\mathbf r(t))x'(t)
        +
        Q(\mathbf r(t))y'(t)
        +
        R(\mathbf r(t))z'(t)\bigr)\,dt.
        \end{aligned}
        }

[53-56 p appE-pull-along-a-curve default_required]
Therefore
      \boxed{\int_C \omega = \int_a^b \mathbf r^*\omega.}

[60-60 title appE-pull-over-a-parametrized-surface default_required]
Pullback over a parametrized surface

[62-69 p appE-pull-over-a-parametrized-surface default_required]
Let
      \mathbf r(u,v)=(x(u,v),y(u,v),z(u,v)).
      Then
      \boxed{\mathbf r^*(dx)=x_u\,du+x_v\,dv.}
      \boxed{\mathbf r^*(dy)=y_u\,du+y_v\,dv.}
      \boxed{\mathbf r^*(dz)=z_u\,du+z_v\,dv.}

[73-73 title appE-pull-of-coordinate-area-forms default_required]
Pullback of coordinate area forms

[75-79 p appE-pull-of-coordinate-area-forms default_required]
\boxed{\mathbf r^*(dx\wedge dy) = (x_u y_v-x_v y_u)\,du\wedge dv.}
      \boxed{\mathbf r^*(dy\wedge dz) = (y_u z_v-y_v z_u)\,du\wedge dv.}
      \boxed{\mathbf r^*(dz\wedge dx) = (z_u x_v-z_v x_u)\,du\wedge dv.}

[83-83 title appE-pull-flux-form default_required]
Flux form pullback

[85-90 p appE-pull-flux-form default_required]
Let
      \Phi_{\mathbf F} = P\,dy\wedge dz + Q\,dz\wedge dx + R\,dx\wedge dy.
      Then
      \boxed{\mathbf r^*\Phi_{\mathbf F} = \mathbf F(\mathbf r(u,v)) \cdot (\mathbf r_u\times\mathbf r_v) \,du\wedge dv.}

[92-95 p appE-pull-flux-form default_required]
Thus
      \boxed{\iint_S \Phi_{\mathbf F} = \iint_D \mathbf F(\mathbf r(u,v)) \cdot (\mathbf r_u\times\mathbf r_v) \,du\,dv.}

[99-99 title appE-pull-under-a-coordinate-change default_required]
Pullback under a coordinate change

[101-106 p appE-pull-under-a-coordinate-change default_required]
Let
      T(u,v)=(x(u,v),y(u,v)).
      Then
      \boxed{T^*(dx\wedge dy) = \frac{\partial(x,y)}{\partial(u,v)} \,du\wedge dv.}

[108-124 p appE-pull-under-a-coordinate-change default_required]
That is,
      
        \boxed{
        \begin{aligned}
        dx\wedge dy
        \amp=
        \det
        \begin{pmatrix}
        x_u\amp x_v\\
        y_u\amp y_v
        \end{pmatrix}
        du\wedge dv.
        \end{aligned}
        }

[126-130 p appE-pull-under-a-coordinate-change default_required]
For a space coordinate change
      T(u,v,w)=(x,y,z),
      \boxed{T^*(dx\wedge dy\wedge dz) = \frac{\partial(x,y,z)}{\partial(u,v,w)} \,du\wedge dv\wedge dw.}

[134-134 title appE-pull-polar default_required]
Polar pullback

[136-140 p appE-pull-polar default_required]
For
      x=r\cos\theta, \qquad y=r\sin\theta,
      \boxed{dx\wedge dy = r\,dr\wedge d\theta.}

[144-144 title appE-pull-cylindrical default_required]
Cylindrical pullback

[146-150 p appE-pull-cylindrical default_required]
For
      x=r\cos\theta, \qquad y=r\sin\theta, \qquad z=z,
      \boxed{dx\wedge dy\wedge dz = r\,dr\wedge d\theta\wedge dz.}

[154-154 title appE-pull-spherical default_required]
Spherical pullback

[156-162 p appE-pull-spherical default_required]
For
      x=\rho\sin\phi\cos\theta,
      y=\rho\sin\phi\sin\theta,
      z=\rho\cos\phi,
      \boxed{dx\wedge dy\wedge dz = \rho^2\sin\phi\, d\rho\wedge d\phi\wedge d\theta.}

[166-166 title appE-pull-common-warnings default_required]
Common warnings

[168-171 p appE-pull-common-warnings default_required]
Pullbacks respect wedge products:
      \boxed{F^*(\alpha\wedge\beta)=F^*\alpha\wedge F^*\beta.}

[173-176 p appE-pull-common-warnings default_required]
Pullbacks respect exterior derivatives:
      \boxed{F^*(d\omega)=d(F^*\omega).}

[179-182 p appE-pull-common-warnings default_required]
Ordinary change of variables uses absolute values for positive area and volume.
        Differential forms keep orientation signs.
