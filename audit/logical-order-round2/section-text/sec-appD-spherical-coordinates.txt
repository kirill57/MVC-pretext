[5-5 title sec-appD-spherical-coordinates default_required]
Spherical coordinates

[8-8 title appD-sph-convention default_required]
Convention used in this book

[10-13 p appD-sph-convention default_required]
Spherical coordinates are
      \boxed{(\rho,\phi,\theta).}

[15-15 p appD-sph-convention default_required]
Here:

[18-18 title appD-sph-tab-convention default_required]
Meaning of the spherical coordinates.

[20-20 cell appD-sph-tab-convention default_required]
\rho

[20-20 cell appD-sph-tab-convention default_required]
distance from the origin

[21-21 cell appD-sph-tab-convention default_required]
\phi

[21-21 cell appD-sph-tab-convention default_required]
angle down from the positive z-axis

[22-22 cell appD-sph-tab-convention default_required]
\theta

[22-22 cell appD-sph-tab-convention default_required]
angle around the z-axis in the xy-plane

[28-28 title appD-sph-conversion-formulas default_required]
Conversion formulas

[30-35 p appD-sph-conversion-formulas default_required]
To Cartesian:
      \boxed{x=\rho\sin\phi\cos\theta}
      \boxed{y=\rho\sin\phi\sin\theta}
      \boxed{z=\rho\cos\phi}

[37-44 p appD-sph-conversion-formulas default_required]
From Cartesian:
      \boxed{\rho^2=x^2+y^2+z^2.}
      \boxed{r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}=\rho\sin\phi.}
      \boxed{z=\rho\cos\phi.}
      \boxed{\tan\theta=\frac yx}
      with quadrant care.

[48-48 title appD-sph-usual-ranges default_required]
Usual ranges

[50-52 p appD-sph-usual-ranges default_required]
\boxed{\rho\ge0, \qquad 0\le\phi\le\pi, \qquad 0\le\theta\lt 2\pi.}

[56-56 title appD-sph-unit-vectors default_required]
Unit vectors

[58-62 p appD-sph-unit-vectors default_required]
\boxed{\widehat{\mathbf e}_\rho = (\sin\phi\cos\theta,\sin\phi\sin\theta,\cos\phi)}
      \boxed{\widehat{\mathbf e}_\phi = (\cos\phi\cos\theta,\cos\phi\sin\theta,-\sin\phi)}
      \boxed{\widehat{\mathbf e}_\theta = (-\sin\theta,\cos\theta,0)}

[64-64 p appD-sph-unit-vectors default_required]
The vector \widehat{\mathbf e}_\rho points outward from the origin.

[66-66 p appD-sph-unit-vectors default_required]
The vector \widehat{\mathbf e}_\phi points in the direction of increasing \phi.

[68-68 p appD-sph-unit-vectors default_required]
The vector \widehat{\mathbf e}_\theta points in the direction of increasing \theta.

[72-72 title appD-sph-small-displacement default_required]
Small displacement

[74-76 p appD-sph-small-displacement default_required]
\boxed{d\mathbf r = \widehat{\mathbf e}_\rho\,d\rho + \widehat{\mathbf e}_\phi\,\rho\,d\phi + \widehat{\mathbf e}_\theta\,\rho\sin\phi\,d\theta.}

[80-80 title appD-sph-line-element default_required]
Line element

[82-84 p appD-sph-line-element default_required]
\boxed{ds^2 = d\rho^2+\rho^2\,d\phi^2+\rho^2\sin^2\phi\,d\theta^2.}

[88-88 title appD-sph-volume-element default_required]
Volume element

[90-93 p appD-sph-volume-element default_required]
Ordinary volume:
      \boxed{dV=\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta.}

[95-98 p appD-sph-volume-element default_required]
Oriented volume:
      \boxed{dx\wedge dy\wedge dz = \rho^2\sin\phi\, d\rho\wedge d\phi\wedge d\theta.}

[102-102 title appD-sph-coordinate-surfaces default_required]
Coordinate surfaces

[104-107 p appD-sph-coordinate-surfaces default_required]
Sphere:
      \boxed{\rho=a.}

[109-112 p appD-sph-coordinate-surfaces default_required]
Cone:
      \boxed{\phi=\phi_0.}

[114-117 p appD-sph-coordinate-surfaces default_required]
Vertical half-plane:
      \boxed{\theta=\theta_0.}

[121-121 title appD-sph-useful-equations default_required]
Useful equations

[123-126 p appD-sph-useful-equations default_required]
Sphere of radius a:
      \boxed{x^2+y^2+z^2=a^2 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \rho=a.}

[128-131 p appD-sph-useful-equations default_required]
Upper cone:
      \boxed{z=\sqrt{x^2+y^2} \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \phi=\frac{\pi}{4}.}

[133-136 p appD-sph-useful-equations default_required]
Plane z=c:
      \boxed{\rho\cos\phi=c.}

[138-141 p appD-sph-useful-equations default_required]
Cylinder x^2+y^2=a^2:
      \boxed{\rho\sin\phi=a.}

[145-145 title appD-sph-spherical-integration default_required]
Spherical integration

[147-149 p appD-sph-spherical-integration default_required]
\boxed{\iiint_E f(x,y,z)\,dV = \iiint_{\widetilde E} f(\rho\sin\phi\cos\theta,\rho\sin\phi\sin\theta,\rho\cos\phi) \rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\,d\phi\,d\theta.}

[153-153 title appD-sph-common-warnings default_required]
Common warnings

[154-158 p appD-sph-common-warnings default_required]
Different books sometimes swap the names \theta and \phi. In this book,
      \phi
      is measured down from the positive z-axis.

[160-160 p appD-sph-common-warnings default_required]
At \rho=0, the angles are not unique.

[162-162 p appD-sph-common-warnings default_required]
At \phi=0 and \phi=\pi, the angle \theta is not unique.

[164-168 p appD-sph-common-warnings default_required]
The factor
      \rho^2\sin\phi
      must not be dropped.
