[5-5 title sec-appA-limits-and-continuity default_required]
Limits and continuity

[8-8 title appA-limits-limits default_required]
Limits

[10-14 p appA-limits-limits default_required]
\boxed{\lim_{x\to a}f(x)=L}
      means that f(x) gets close to L as x gets close to a, with
      x\ne a.

[16-16 p appA-limits-limits default_required]
The value f(a) does not have to exist.

[18-22 p appA-limits-limits default_required]
\lim_{x\to a}f(x)
      exists exactly when the two one-sided limits agree:
      \boxed{\lim_{x\to a^-}f(x)=\lim_{x\to a^+}f(x)=L.}

[26-26 title appA-limits-common-limit-laws default_required]
Common limit laws

[28-34 p appA-limits-common-limit-laws default_required]
If the limits exist, then
      \lim_{x\to a}(f+g)=\lim_{x\to a}f+\lim_{x\to a}g,
      \lim_{x\to a}(fg)=\left(\lim_{x\to a}f\right)\left(\lim_{x\to a}g\right),
      and, if \lim_{x\to a}g(x)\ne0,
      \lim_{x\to a}\frac{f}{g}=\frac{\lim_{x\to a}f}{\lim_{x\to a}g}.

[38-38 title appA-limits-useful-standard-limits default_required]
Useful standard limits

[40-42 p appA-limits-useful-standard-limits default_required]
\boxed{\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin x}{x}=1}

[44-46 p appA-limits-useful-standard-limits default_required]
\boxed{\lim_{x\to0}\frac{1-\cos x}{x}=0}

[48-50 p appA-limits-useful-standard-limits default_required]
\boxed{\lim_{x\to0}\frac{e^x-1}{x}=1}

[52-54 p appA-limits-useful-standard-limits default_required]
\boxed{\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\ln(1+x)}{x}=1}

[58-58 title appA-limits-continuity default_required]
Continuity

[60-63 p appA-limits-continuity default_required]
A function f is continuous at a if
      \boxed{\lim_{x\to a}f(x)=f(a).}

[65-71 p appA-limits-continuity default_required]
This requires three things:
      f(a)\text{ is defined},
      \lim_{x\to a}f(x)\text{ exists},
      and
      \lim_{x\to a}f(x)=f(a).

[75-75 title appA-limits-common-continuous-functions default_required]
Common continuous functions

[77-77 p appA-limits-common-continuous-functions default_required]
Polynomials are continuous everywhere.

[79-79 p appA-limits-common-continuous-functions default_required]
Rational functions are continuous where their denominators are nonzero.

[81-85 p appA-limits-common-continuous-functions default_required]
The functions
      \sin x,\qquad \cos x,\qquad e^x
      are continuous everywhere.

[87-92 p appA-limits-common-continuous-functions default_required]
The function
      \ln x
      is continuous for
      x\gt0.

[94-94 p appA-limits-common-continuous-functions default_required]
Compositions of continuous functions are continuous where they are defined.

[98-98 title appA-limits-interval-theorems default_required]
Interval theorems

[101-101 title appA-limits-thm-intermediate-value default_required]
Intermediate Value Theorem

[103-107 p appA-limits-thm-intermediate-value default_required]
If f is continuous on [a,b], and N lies between f(a) and
          f(b), then there is some c\in[a,b] such that
          f(c)=N.

[112-112 title appA-limits-thm-extreme-value default_required]
Extreme Value Theorem

[114-117 p appA-limits-thm-extreme-value default_required]
If f is continuous on a closed interval [a,b], then f has an
          absolute maximum and an absolute minimum on [a,b].

[123-123 title appA-limits-warnings default_required]
Warnings

[125-125 p appA-limits-warnings default_required]
A limit can exist even if f(a) is missing.

[127-127 p appA-limits-warnings default_required]
A function can be defined at a but still fail to be continuous there.

[129-129 p appA-limits-warnings default_required]
Continuity on an open interval does not guarantee an absolute maximum or minimum.

[131-131 p appA-limits-warnings default_required]
In several variables, checking only one path is not enough to prove a limit exists.
