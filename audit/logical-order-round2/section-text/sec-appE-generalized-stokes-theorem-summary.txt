[5-5 title sec-appE-generalized-stokes-theorem-summary default_required]
Generalized Stokes theorem summary

[8-8 title appE-gstokes-the-theorem default_required]
The theorem

[10-18 p appE-gstokes-the-theorem default_required]
\boxed{
            \int_{\partial M}\omega
            =
            \int_M d\omega.
        }

[20-20 p appE-gstokes-the-theorem default_required]
Here M is compact and oriented with piecewise smooth boundary, and \omega is a C^1 form of degree \dim M-1 on an open neighborhood of M. The boundary has the induced orientation. This is the generalized Stokes theorem.

[22-22 p appE-gstokes-the-theorem default_required]
It says:

[24-32 p appE-gstokes-the-theorem default_required]
\boxed{
            \text{the integral of a form over the boundary}
            =
            \text{the integral of its derivative over the region}.
        }

Degree matching

[38-41 p appE-gstokes-degree-matching default_required]

      d\omega is a k-form.

[58-58 title appE-gstokes-fundamental-theorem-of-calculus default_required]
Fundamental Theorem of Calculus

[60-63 p appE-gstokes-fundamental-theorem-of-calculus default_required]
Let M=[a,b], and let \omega=f. Then
      d\omega=df=f'(x)\,dx.

[65-72 p appE-gstokes-fundamental-theorem-of-calculus default_required]
Generalized Stokes gives
      
        \boxed{
            \int_a^b f'(x)\,dx=f(b)-f(a).
        }

[74-81 p appE-gstokes-fundamental-theorem-of-calculus default_required]
Boundary:
      
        \boxed{
            \partial[a,b]=\{b\}-\{a\}.
        }

[85-85 title appE-gstokes-greens-theorem-circulation-form default_required]
Green's theorem: circulation form

[87-90 p appE-gstokes-greens-theorem-circulation-form default_required]
Let
      \omega=P\,dx+Q\,dy.

[92-95 p appE-gstokes-greens-theorem-circulation-form default_required]
Then
      d\omega=(Q_x-P_y)\,dx\wedge dy.

[97-106 p appE-gstokes-greens-theorem-circulation-form default_required]
Stokes gives
      
        \boxed{
            \int_{\partial R}P\,dx+Q\,dy
            =
            \iint_R (Q_x-P_y)\,dx\wedge dy.
        }

[108-117 p appE-gstokes-greens-theorem-circulation-form default_required]
Classical notation:
      
        \boxed{
            \oint_{\partial R}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r
            =
            \iint_R \operatorname{curl}_{\mathrm{plane}}\mathbf F\,dA.
        }

[121-121 title appE-gstokes-greens-theorem-flux-form default_required]
Green's theorem: flux form

[123-128 p appE-gstokes-greens-theorem-flux-form default_required]
Let
      \mathbf F=(M,N),
      and let
      \phi_{\mathbf F}=M\,dy-N\,dx.

[130-133 p appE-gstokes-greens-theorem-flux-form default_required]
Then
      d\phi_{\mathbf F}=(M_x+N_y)\,dx\wedge dy.

[135-144 p appE-gstokes-greens-theorem-flux-form default_required]
Stokes gives
      
        \boxed{
            \int_{\partial R}M\,dy-N\,dx
            =
            \iint_R (M_x+N_y)\,dx\wedge dy.
        }

[146-155 p appE-gstokes-greens-theorem-flux-form default_required]
Classical notation:
      
        \boxed{
            \int_{\partial R}\mathbf F\cdot\widehat{\mathbf n}\,ds
            =
            \iint_R \nabla\cdot\mathbf F\,dA.
        }

[159-159 title appE-gstokes-stokes-theorem-in-space default_required]
Stokes' theorem in space

[161-164 p appE-gstokes-stokes-theorem-in-space default_required]
Let
      \omega=P\,dx+Q\,dy+R\,dz.

[166-169 p appE-gstokes-stokes-theorem-in-space default_required]
Then
      d\omega=\Phi_{\nabla\times\mathbf F}.

[171-180 p appE-gstokes-stokes-theorem-in-space default_required]
Stokes gives
      
        \boxed{
            \int_{\partial S}\omega
            =
            \int_S d\omega.
        }

[182-191 p appE-gstokes-stokes-theorem-in-space default_required]
Classical notation:
      
        \boxed{
            \oint_{\partial S}\mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r
            =
            \iint_S(\nabla\times\mathbf F)\cdot\widehat{\mathbf n}\,dS.
        }

[195-195 title appE-gstokes-divergence-theorem default_required]
Divergence theorem

[197-204 p appE-gstokes-divergence-theorem default_required]
Let
      
        \Phi_{\mathbf F}
        =
        P\,dy\wedge dz+Q\,dz\wedge dx+R\,dx\wedge dy.

[206-213 p appE-gstokes-divergence-theorem default_required]
Then
      
        d\Phi_{\mathbf F}
        =
        (P_x+Q_y+R_z)\,dx\wedge dy\wedge dz.

[215-224 p appE-gstokes-divergence-theorem default_required]
Stokes gives
      
        \boxed{
            \int_{\partial E}\Phi_{\mathbf F}
            =
            \int_E d\Phi_{\mathbf F}.
        }

[226-235 p appE-gstokes-divergence-theorem default_required]
Classical notation:
      
        \boxed{
            \iint_{\partial E}\mathbf F\cdot\widehat{\mathbf n}\,dS
            =
            \iiint_E\nabla\cdot\mathbf F\,dV.
        }

[239-239 title appE-gstokes-orientation-rules default_required]
Orientation rules

[241-248 p appE-gstokes-orientation-rules default_required]
Interval:
      
        \boxed{
            \partial[a,b]=\{b\}-\{a\}.
        }

[250-257 p appE-gstokes-orientation-rules default_required]
Plane region:
      
        \boxed{
            \text{walk so the region stays on your left.}
        }

[259-266 p appE-gstokes-orientation-rules default_required]
Region with a hole:
      
        \boxed{
            \text{outer boundary counterclockwise, inner boundary clockwise.}
        }

[268-275 p appE-gstokes-orientation-rules default_required]
Surface:
      
        \boxed{
            \text{use the right-hand rule from the chosen normal.}
        }

[277-284 p appE-gstokes-orientation-rules default_required]
Solid:
      
        \boxed{
            \text{boundary is oriented outward.}
        }

[288-288 title appE-gstokes-hypotheses-checklist default_required]
Hypotheses checklist

[290-290 p appE-gstokes-hypotheses-checklist default_required]
Before using Stokes' theorem, check:

[293-293 li appE-gstokes-hypotheses-checklist default_required]
The form is defined on the region and its boundary.

[294-294 li appE-gstokes-hypotheses-checklist default_required]
The needed partial derivatives exist and are continuous.

[295-295 li appE-gstokes-hypotheses-checklist default_required]
The region is oriented.

[296-296 li appE-gstokes-hypotheses-checklist default_required]
The boundary has the induced orientation.

[297-297 li appE-gstokes-hypotheses-checklist default_required]
Singularities are not inside the region unless handled separately.

[298-298 li appE-gstokes-hypotheses-checklist default_required]
The surface or region is piecewise smooth.

[303-303 title appE-gstokes-closed-versus-exact-warning default_required]
Closed versus exact warning

[306-311 p appE-gstokes-closed-versus-exact-warning default_required]
If
        \omega=d\eta,
        then
        d\omega=0.

[313-313 p appE-gstokes-closed-versus-exact-warning default_required]
So exact implies closed.

[315-315 p appE-gstokes-closed-versus-exact-warning default_required]
But closed does not always imply exact.

[317-319 p appE-gstokes-closed-versus-exact-warning default_required]
A missing point or hole in the domain can prevent a potential from existing globally.

[324-324 title appE-gstokes-boundary-of-a-boundary default_required]
Boundary of a boundary

[326-333 p appE-gstokes-boundary-of-a-boundary default_required]
A boundary has no boundary:
      
        \boxed{
            \partial(\partial M)=0.
        }

[335-342 p appE-gstokes-boundary-of-a-boundary default_required]
The form version is
      
        \boxed{
            d^2=0.
        }

[344-344 p appE-gstokes-boundary-of-a-boundary default_required]
These are two sides of the same idea.
