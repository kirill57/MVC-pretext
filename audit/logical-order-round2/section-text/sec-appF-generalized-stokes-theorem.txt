[5-5 title sec-appF-generalized-stokes-theorem default_required]
Generalized Stokes theorem

[8-8 title appF-gstokes-statement default_required]
Statement

[10-20 p appF-gstokes-statement default_required]
For an oriented k-dimensional region M and a (k-1)-form
      \omega,
      
      \boxed{
          \int_{\partial M}\omega
          =
          \int_M d\omega.
      }

[22-22 p appF-gstokes-statement default_required]
This single statement includes:

[24-33 p appF-gstokes-statement default_required]
\text{Theorem} \amp M \amp \omega
        \hline
        \text{FTC} \amp \text{interval} \amp 0\text{-form}
        \text{Green} \amp \text{plane region} \amp 1\text{-form}
        \text{Stokes} \amp \text{surface} \amp 1\text{-form}
        \text{Divergence} \amp \text{solid} \amp 2\text{-form}

[37-37 title appF-gstokes-degree-matching default_required]
Degree matching

[39-47 p appF-gstokes-degree-matching default_required]
If
      \dim M=k,
      then
      \omega
      must be a (k-1)-form and
      d\omega
      is a k-form.

[49-57 p appF-gstokes-degree-matching default_required]
\boxed{
          \int_{\partial M}\omega
      }
      
      makes sense because
      \dim(\partial M)=k-1.

[59-66 p appF-gstokes-degree-matching default_required]
\boxed{
          \int_M d\omega
      }
      
      makes sense because d\omega has degree k.

[70-70 title appF-gstokes-hypotheses default_required]
Hypotheses

[72-72 p appF-gstokes-hypotheses default_required]
The clean version assumes:

[74-81 p appF-gstokes-hypotheses default_required]
1. \amp M\text{ is compact, oriented, and piecewise smooth.}
        2. \amp \partial M\text{ has the induced boundary orientation.}
        3. \amp \omega\text{ is }C^1\text{ on an open neighborhood of }M.
        4. \amp \text{The integrals are defined.}

[85-85 title appF-gstokes-proof-idea default_required]
Proof idea

[87-87 p appF-gstokes-proof-idea default_required]
Break M into many small oriented pieces.

[89-89 p appF-gstokes-proof-idea default_required]
On each small piece, the theorem reduces to the coordinate version.

[91-94 p appF-gstokes-proof-idea default_required]
When the small pieces are added, every interior boundary appears twice with opposite
      orientations.

[96-96 p appF-gstokes-proof-idea default_required]
Those interior contributions cancel.

[98-98 p appF-gstokes-proof-idea default_required]
Only the boundary of the whole region remains.

[100-102 p appF-gstokes-proof-idea default_required]
This is the same cancellation pattern behind Green's theorem and the divergence theorem.

[106-106 title appF-gstokes-dependencies default_required]
Dependencies

[108-117 p appF-gstokes-dependencies default_required]
1. \amp \text{The one-dimensional FTC on coordinate intervals.}
        2. \amp \text{Wedge products and orientation.}
        3. \amp \text{Exterior derivative.}
        4. \amp \text{Pullbacks to parametrized pieces.}
        5. \amp \text{Boundary orientation conventions.}
        6. \amp \text{Cancellation of interior boundaries.}

[121-121 title appF-gstokes-boundary-of-a-boundary default_required]
Boundary of a boundary

[123-130 p appF-gstokes-boundary-of-a-boundary default_required]
A boundary has no boundary:
      
      \boxed{
          \partial(\partial M)=0.
      }

[132-139 p appF-gstokes-boundary-of-a-boundary default_required]
The form version is
      
      \boxed{
          d^2=0.
      }

[141-141 p appF-gstokes-boundary-of-a-boundary default_required]
These two facts match.

[143-148 p appF-gstokes-boundary-of-a-boundary default_required]
If
      \partial(\partial M)=0,
      then for a C^2 form \omega of degree k-2, applying Stokes twice gives
      \int_{\partial(\partial M)}\omega=0.

[150-153 p appF-gstokes-boundary-of-a-boundary default_required]
On the derivative side,
      \int_M d(d\omega)=\int_M 0=0.

[157-157 title appF-gstokes-closed-and-exact default_required]
Closed and exact

[159-166 p appF-gstokes-closed-and-exact default_required]
A form is closed if
      
      \boxed{
          d\omega=0.
      }

[168-175 p appF-gstokes-closed-and-exact default_required]
A form is exact if
      
      \boxed{
          \omega=d\eta.
      }

[177-186 p appF-gstokes-closed-and-exact default_required]
Exact implies closed:
      
      \boxed{
          \omega=d\eta
          \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
          d\omega=d^2\eta=0.
      }

[188-188 p appF-gstokes-closed-and-exact default_required]
Closed need not imply exact.

[190-190 p appF-gstokes-closed-and-exact default_required]
A hole in the domain can prevent exactness.

[194-194 title appF-gstokes-what-can-fail default_required]
What can fail

[196-196 p appF-gstokes-what-can-fail default_required]
If the form is not defined on all of M, Stokes' theorem may fail.

[198-198 p appF-gstokes-what-can-fail default_required]
If the orientation of \partial M is wrong, the sign is wrong.

[200-203 p appF-gstokes-what-can-fail default_required]
If M has corners, edges, or several patches, it must be handled as a piecewise
      smooth object with consistent orientations.

[205-208 p appF-gstokes-what-can-fail default_required]
If singularities are inside the region, remove small neighborhoods around them and include
      the new boundary pieces.

[212-212 title appF-gstokes-moral default_required]
Moral

[214-214 p appF-gstokes-moral default_required]
Every version of Stokes' theorem says the same thing:

[216-224 p appF-gstokes-moral default_required]
\boxed{
          \text{local derivative information inside}
          =
          \text{accumulated boundary information}.
      }

[226-229 p appF-gstokes-moral default_required]
The shape of the region changes from interval to surface to solid, but the mechanism is
      always the same: orient, differentiate, integrate, and cancel interior boundaries.
