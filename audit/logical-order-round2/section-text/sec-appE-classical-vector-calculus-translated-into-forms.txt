[5-5 title sec-appE-classical-vector-calculus-translated-into-forms default_required]
Classical vector calculus translated into forms

[8-8 title appE-trans-gradient default_required]
Gradient

[10-13 p appE-trans-gradient default_required]
Classical:
      \nabla f=(f_x,f_y,f_z).

[15-18 p appE-trans-gradient default_required]
Forms:
      df=f_x\,dx+f_y\,dy+f_z\,dz.

[20-23 p appE-trans-gradient default_required]
Translation:
      df(\mathbf v)=\nabla f\cdot \mathbf v.

[27-27 title appE-trans-work-form default_required]
Work form

[29-34 p appE-trans-work-form default_required]
For
      \mathbf F=(P,Q,R),
      classical line integral:
      \int_C \mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r.

[36-39 p appE-trans-work-form default_required]
Forms:
      \omega_{\mathbf F}=P\,dx+Q\,dy+R\,dz.

[41-47 p appE-trans-work-form default_required]
\int_C \mathbf F\cdot d\mathbf r
        =
        \int_C \omega_{\mathbf F}.

[51-51 title appE-trans-curl default_required]
Curl

[53-60 p appE-trans-curl default_required]
Classical:
      
        \nabla\times\mathbf F
        =
        (R_y-Q_z,\ P_z-R_x,\ Q_x-P_y).

[62-73 p appE-trans-curl default_required]
Forms:
      
        d\omega_{\mathbf F}
        =
        (R_y-Q_z)\,dy\wedge dz
        +
        (P_z-R_x)\,dz\wedge dx
        +
        (Q_x-P_y)\,dx\wedge dy.

[75-82 p appE-trans-curl default_required]
Translation:
      
        d\omega_{\mathbf F}
        =
        \Phi_{\nabla\times\mathbf F}.

[86-86 title appE-trans-flux-form default_required]
Flux form

[88-93 p appE-trans-flux-form default_required]
For
      \mathbf F=(P,Q,R),
      classical flux:
      \iint_S \mathbf F\cdot\widehat{\mathbf n}\,dS.

[95-106 p appE-trans-flux-form default_required]
Forms:
      
        \Phi_{\mathbf F}
        =
        P\,dy\wedge dz
        +
        Q\,dz\wedge dx
        +
        R\,dx\wedge dy.

[108-114 p appE-trans-flux-form default_required]
\iint_S \mathbf F\cdot\widehat{\mathbf n}\,dS
        =
        \iint_S \Phi_{\mathbf F}.

[118-118 title appE-trans-divergence default_required]
Divergence

[120-123 p appE-trans-divergence default_required]
Classical:
      \nabla\cdot\mathbf F=P_x+Q_y+R_z.

[125-132 p appE-trans-divergence default_required]
Forms:
      
        d\Phi_{\mathbf F}
        =
        (P_x+Q_y+R_z)\,dx\wedge dy\wedge dz.

[134-141 p appE-trans-divergence default_required]
Translation:
      
        d\Phi_{\mathbf F}
        =
        (\nabla\cdot\mathbf F)\,dx\wedge dy\wedge dz.

[145-145 title appE-trans-plane-circulation default_required]
Plane circulation

[147-152 p appE-trans-plane-circulation default_required]
For
      \mathbf F=(P,Q),
      work form:
      \omega=P\,dx+Q\,dy.

[154-157 p appE-trans-plane-circulation default_required]
Scalar curl:
      \operatorname{curl}_{\mathrm{plane}}\mathbf F=Q_x-P_y.

[159-162 p appE-trans-plane-circulation default_required]
Forms:
      d\omega=(Q_x-P_y)\,dx\wedge dy.

[166-166 title appE-trans-plane-flux default_required]
Plane flux

[168-173 p appE-trans-plane-flux default_required]
For
      \mathbf F=(M,N),
      the outward flux form on a positively oriented boundary is
      \phi_{\mathbf F}=M\,dy-N\,dx.

[175-182 p appE-trans-plane-flux default_required]
Then
      
        \int_{\partial R}\phi_{\mathbf F}
        =
        \int_{\partial R}\mathbf F\cdot\widehat{\mathbf n}\,ds.

[184-191 p appE-trans-plane-flux default_required]
Its exterior derivative is
      
        d\phi_{\mathbf F}
        =
        (M_x+N_y)\,dx\wedge dy.

[193-200 p appE-trans-plane-flux default_required]
So
      
        d\phi_{\mathbf F}
        =
        (\nabla\cdot\mathbf F)\,dx\wedge dy.

[204-204 title appE-trans-vector-identities-d-squared-zero default_required]
Vector identities from d^2=0

[206-211 p appE-trans-vector-identities-d-squared-zero default_required]
From
      d(df)=0,
      we get
      \nabla\times(\nabla f)=\mathbf 0.

[213-218 p appE-trans-vector-identities-d-squared-zero default_required]
From
      d(d\omega_{\mathbf F})=0,
      we get
      \nabla\cdot(\nabla\times\mathbf F)=0.

[262-262 title appE-trans-common-warnings default_required]
Common warnings

[263-273 p appE-trans-common-warnings default_required]
The same vector field \mathbf F can produce two different forms:
      \omega_{\mathbf F}=P\,dx+Q\,dy+R\,dz
      for work and circulation.
      
        \Phi_{\mathbf F}
        =
        P\,dy\wedge dz+Q\,dz\wedge dx+R\,dx\wedge dy
      
      for flux.

[274-274 p appE-trans-common-warnings default_required]
Do not confuse these.
