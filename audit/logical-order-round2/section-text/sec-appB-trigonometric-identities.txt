Unit circle

[10-14 p appB-trig-unit-circle default_required]

[129-129 title appB-trig-other-functions default_required]
Other trigonometric functions

[131-133 p appB-trig-other-functions default_required]
\tan\theta=\frac{\sin\theta}{\cos\theta}

[135-137 p appB-trig-other-functions default_required]
\sec\theta=\frac1{\cos\theta}

[139-141 p appB-trig-other-functions default_required]
\csc\theta=\frac1{\sin\theta}

[143-145 p appB-trig-other-functions default_required]
\cot\theta=\frac{\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}

[149-149 title appB-trig-pythagorean-identities default_required]
Pythagorean identities

[151-153 p appB-trig-pythagorean-identities default_required]
\sin^2\theta+\cos^2\theta=1

[155-157 p appB-trig-pythagorean-identities default_required]
1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta

[159-161 p appB-trig-pythagorean-identities default_required]
1+\cot^2\theta=\csc^2\theta

[165-165 title appB-trig-even-and-odd-identities default_required]
Even and odd identities

[167-169 p appB-trig-even-and-odd-identities default_required]
\cos(-\theta)=\cos\theta

[171-173 p appB-trig-even-and-odd-identities default_required]
\sin(-\theta)=-\sin\theta

[175-177 p appB-trig-even-and-odd-identities default_required]
\tan(-\theta)=-\tan\theta

[181-181 title appB-trig-periodicity default_required]
Periodicity

[183-185 p appB-trig-periodicity default_required]
\sin(\theta+2\pi)=\sin\theta

[187-189 p appB-trig-periodicity default_required]
\cos(\theta+2\pi)=\cos\theta

[191-193 p appB-trig-periodicity default_required]
\tan(\theta+\pi)=\tan\theta

[197-197 title appB-trig-angle-addition-formulas default_required]
Angle addition formulas

[199-201 p appB-trig-angle-addition-formulas default_required]
\cos(\alpha+\beta)=\cos\alpha\cos\beta-\sin\alpha\sin\beta

[203-205 p appB-trig-angle-addition-formulas default_required]
\sin(\alpha+\beta)=\sin\alpha\cos\beta+\cos\alpha\sin\beta

[207-209 p appB-trig-angle-addition-formulas default_required]
\cos(\alpha-\beta)=\cos\alpha\cos\beta+\sin\alpha\sin\beta

[211-213 p appB-trig-angle-addition-formulas default_required]
\sin(\alpha-\beta)=\sin\alpha\cos\beta-\cos\alpha\sin\beta

[217-217 title appB-trig-double-angle-formulas default_required]
Double-angle formulas

[219-221 p appB-trig-double-angle-formulas default_required]
\sin(2\theta)=2\sin\theta\cos\theta

[223-225 p appB-trig-double-angle-formulas default_required]
\cos(2\theta)=\cos^2\theta-\sin^2\theta

[227-229 p appB-trig-double-angle-formulas default_required]
\cos(2\theta)=2\cos^2\theta-1

[231-233 p appB-trig-double-angle-formulas default_required]
\cos(2\theta)=1-2\sin^2\theta

[237-237 title appB-trig-half-angle-identities default_required]
Half-angle identities

[239-241 p appB-trig-half-angle-identities default_required]
\cos^2\theta=\frac{1+\cos(2\theta)}{2}

[243-245 p appB-trig-half-angle-identities default_required]
\sin^2\theta=\frac{1-\cos(2\theta)}{2}

[269-269 title appB-trig-inverse-trig-derivatives default_required]
Inverse trig derivatives

[271-273 p appB-trig-inverse-trig-derivatives default_required]
\frac{d}{dx}\sin^{-1}x=\frac1{\sqrt{1-x^2}}

[275-277 p appB-trig-inverse-trig-derivatives default_required]
\frac{d}{dx}\cos^{-1}x=-\frac1{\sqrt{1-x^2}}

[279-281 p appB-trig-inverse-trig-derivatives default_required]
\frac{d}{dx}\tan^{-1}x=\frac1{1+x^2}

[285-285 title appB-trig-polar-conversion default_required]
Polar conversion

[287-289 p appB-trig-polar-conversion default_required]
x=r\cos\theta,\qquad y=r\sin\theta.

[291-293 p appB-trig-polar-conversion default_required]
r^2=x^2+y^2.

[295-298 p appB-trig-polar-conversion default_required]
\tan\theta=\frac yx
      with quadrant care.

[302-302 title appB-trig-common-warnings default_required]
Common warnings

[305-305 p appB-trig-common-warnings default_required]
Radians are the default in calculus.

[307-313 p appB-trig-common-warnings default_required]
\sin^2\theta
        means
        (\sin\theta)^2,
        not
        \sin(\theta^2).

[315-319 p appB-trig-common-warnings default_required]
The equation
        \tan\theta=\frac yx
        does not determine the quadrant by itself.
