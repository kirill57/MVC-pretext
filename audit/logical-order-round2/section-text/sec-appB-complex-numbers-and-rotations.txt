[5-5 title sec-appB-complex-numbers-and-rotations default_required]
Complex numbers and rotations

[8-8 title appB-cplx-subsec-complex-numbers default_required]
Complex numbers

[10-14 p appB-cplx-subsec-complex-numbers default_required]
\boxed{z=a+bi}
      where
      \boxed{i^2=-1.}

[16-19 p appB-cplx-subsec-complex-numbers default_required]
Real part:
      \boxed{\operatorname{Re}(a+bi)=a.}

[21-24 p appB-cplx-subsec-complex-numbers default_required]
Imaginary part:
      \boxed{\operatorname{Im}(a+bi)=b.}

[26-29 p appB-cplx-subsec-complex-numbers default_required]
Plane point:
      \boxed{a+bi \longleftrightarrow (a,b).}

[33-33 title appB-cplx-subsec-addition-and-subtraction default_required]
Addition and subtraction

[35-37 p appB-cplx-subsec-addition-and-subtraction default_required]
\boxed{(a+bi)+(c+di)=(a+c)+(b+d)i.}

[39-41 p appB-cplx-subsec-addition-and-subtraction default_required]
\boxed{(a+bi)-(c+di)=(a-c)+(b-d)i.}

[45-45 title appB-cplx-subsec-multiplication default_required]
Multiplication

[47-49 p appB-cplx-subsec-multiplication default_required]
\boxed{(a+bi)(c+di)=(ac-bd)+(ad+bc)i.}

[51-54 p appB-cplx-subsec-multiplication default_required]
Multiplication by i:
      \boxed{i(a+bi)=-b+ai.}

[56-60 p appB-cplx-subsec-multiplication default_required]
As a plane rotation:
      \boxed{(a,b)\mapsto(-b,a).}
      This is a 90^\circ counterclockwise rotation.

[64-64 title appB-cplx-subsec-conjugate default_required]
Conjugate

[66-68 p appB-cplx-subsec-conjugate default_required]
\boxed{\overline{a+bi}=a-bi.}

[70-73 p appB-cplx-subsec-conjugate default_required]
Geometrically, conjugation reflects across the real axis:
      (a,b)\mapsto(a,-b).

[77-77 title appB-cplx-subsec-modulus default_required]
Modulus

[79-81 p appB-cplx-subsec-modulus default_required]
\boxed{|a+bi|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}.}

[83-85 p appB-cplx-subsec-modulus default_required]
\boxed{z\overline z=|z|^2.}

[89-89 title appB-cplx-subsec-division default_required]
Division

[91-96 p appB-cplx-subsec-division default_required]
To divide by
      c+di,
      multiply top and bottom by
      c-di.

[98-102 p appB-cplx-subsec-division default_required]
\boxed{\frac{a+bi}{c+di} = \frac{(a+bi)(c-di)}{c^2+d^2}}
      for
      c+di\ne0.

[106-106 title appB-cplx-subsec-polar-form default_required]
Polar form

[108-115 p appB-cplx-subsec-polar-form default_required]
If
      z=a+bi\ne0,
      then
      \boxed{z=r(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)}
      where
      \boxed{r=|z|=\sqrt{a^2+b^2}.}

[117-120 p appB-cplx-subsec-polar-form default_required]
The angle \theta satisfies
      \cos\theta=\frac ar, \qquad \sin\theta=\frac br.

[124-124 title appB-cplx-subsec-multiplication-in-polar-form default_required]
Multiplication in polar form

[126-133 p appB-cplx-subsec-multiplication-in-polar-form default_required]
If
      z=r(\cos\alpha+i\sin\alpha),
      and
      w=s(\cos\beta+i\sin\beta),
      then
      \boxed{zw = rs\bigl(\cos(\alpha+\beta)+i\sin(\alpha+\beta)\bigr).}

[135-138 p appB-cplx-subsec-multiplication-in-polar-form default_required]
Complex multiplication:
      \boxed{\text{multiplies lengths and adds angles.}}

[142-142 title appB-cplx-subsec-de-moivres-formula default_required]
De Moivre's formula

[144-146 p appB-cplx-subsec-de-moivres-formula default_required]
\boxed{\bigl(r(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)\bigr)^n = r^n(\cos(n\theta)+i\sin(n\theta)).}

[148-151 p appB-cplx-subsec-de-moivres-formula default_required]
For unit complex numbers:
      \boxed{(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)^n = \cos(n\theta)+i\sin(n\theta).}

[155-155 title appB-cplx-warning-common-warnings default_required]
Common warnings

[156-158 p appB-cplx-warning-common-warnings default_required]
The number 0 has no polar angle.

[159-163 p appB-cplx-warning-common-warnings default_required]
Angles in polar form are not unique:
      \theta \quad\text{and}\quad \theta+2\pi k
      describe the same direction.

[164-167 p appB-cplx-warning-common-warnings default_required]
Complex multiplication is not componentwise multiplication:
      (a,b)(c,d)\ne(ac,bd).
