[8-8 title appD-elem-scale-factors default_required]
Scale factors

[10-15 p appD-elem-scale-factors default_required]
For orthogonal coordinates
      (u_1,u_2,u_3),
      the scale factors are
      \boxed{h_i=\left|\frac{\partial\mathbf r}{\partial u_i}\right|.}

[17-20 p appD-elem-scale-factors default_required]
The line element is
      \boxed{ds^2=h_1^2\,du_1^2+h_2^2\,du_2^2+h_3^2\,du_3^2.}

[22-25 p appD-elem-scale-factors default_required]
The volume element is
      \boxed{dV=h_1h_2h_3\,du_1\,du_2\,du_3.}

[59-59 title appD-elem-parametric-surface default_required]
Parametric surface

[61-66 p appD-elem-parametric-surface default_required]
For a surface
      \mathbf r(u,v),
      the vector area element is
      \boxed{d\mathbf S=(\mathbf r_u\times\mathbf r_v)\,du\,dv.}

[68-71 p appD-elem-parametric-surface default_required]
The scalar surface area element is
      \boxed{dS=|\mathbf r_u\times\mathbf r_v|\,du\,dv.}

[73-76 p appD-elem-parametric-surface default_required]
Flux through the oriented surface is
      \boxed{\iint_S \mathbf F\cdot\widehat{\mathbf n}\,dS=\iint_D \mathbf F(\mathbf r(u,v))\cdot(\mathbf r_u\times\mathbf r_v)\,du\,dv.}

[80-80 title appD-elem-graph-surface default_required]
Graph surface

[82-87 p appD-elem-graph-surface default_required]
For a graph
      z=f(x,y),
      use
      \mathbf r(x,y)=(x,y,f(x,y)).

[89-92 p appD-elem-graph-surface default_required]
Then
      \boxed{\mathbf r_x\times\mathbf r_y=(-f_x,-f_y,1).}

[94-97 p appD-elem-graph-surface default_required]
Upward-oriented vector area:
      \boxed{d\mathbf S=(-f_x,-f_y,1)\,dx\,dy.}

[99-102 p appD-elem-graph-surface default_required]
Surface area element:
      \boxed{dS=\sqrt{1+f_x^2+f_y^2}\,dx\,dy.}

[106-106 title appD-elem-polar-area default_required]
Polar area

[108-110 p appD-elem-polar-area default_required]
\boxed{dA=r\,dr\,d\theta.}

[112-117 p appD-elem-polar-area default_required]
For a polar curve
      r=f(\theta),
      area traced once:
      \boxed{A=\frac12\int_\alpha^\beta f(\theta)^2\,d\theta.}

[121-121 title appD-elem-cylindrical-surface-elements default_required]
Cylindrical surface elements

[123-128 p appD-elem-cylindrical-surface-elements default_required]
For the cylinder
      r=a,
      with parameters (\theta,z),
      \boxed{dS=a\,d\theta\,dz.}

[130-135 p appD-elem-cylindrical-surface-elements default_required]
For the plane
      z=c,
      with polar parameters (r,\theta),
      \boxed{dS=r\,dr\,d\theta.}

[137-142 p appD-elem-cylindrical-surface-elements default_required]
For the half-plane
      \theta=\theta_0,
      with parameters (r,z),
      \boxed{dS=dr\,dz.}

[146-146 title appD-elem-spherical-surface-elements default_required]
Spherical surface elements

[148-153 p appD-elem-spherical-surface-elements default_required]
For the sphere
      \rho=a,
      with parameters (\phi,\theta),
      \boxed{dS=a^2\sin\phi\,d\phi\,d\theta.}

[155-160 p appD-elem-spherical-surface-elements default_required]
For the cone
      \phi=\phi_0,
      with parameters (\rho,\theta),
      \boxed{dS=\rho\sin\phi_0\,d\rho\,d\theta.}

[162-167 p appD-elem-spherical-surface-elements default_required]
For the half-plane
      \theta=\theta_0,
      with parameters (\rho,\phi),
      \boxed{dS=\rho\,d\rho\,d\phi.}

[171-171 title appD-elem-boundary-orientation-reminders default_required]
Boundary orientation reminders

[173-176 p appD-elem-boundary-orientation-reminders default_required]
In the plane, positive boundary orientation means:
      \boxed{\text{walk so the region stays on your left.}}

[178-180 p appD-elem-boundary-orientation-reminders default_required]
For an upward-oriented surface, use the right-hand rule to orient the boundary.

[182-184 p appD-elem-boundary-orientation-reminders default_required]
For a solid, the positive boundary orientation is outward.

[188-188 title appD-elem-forms-version-common-elements default_required]
Forms version of common elements

[190-193 p appD-elem-forms-version-common-elements default_required]
Polar:
      \boxed{dx\wedge dy=r\,dr\wedge d\theta.}

[195-198 p appD-elem-forms-version-common-elements default_required]
Cylindrical:
      \boxed{dx\wedge dy\wedge dz=r\,dr\wedge d\theta\wedge dz.}

[200-203 p appD-elem-forms-version-common-elements default_required]
Spherical:
      \boxed{dx\wedge dy\wedge dz=\rho^2\sin\phi\,d\rho\wedge d\phi\wedge d\theta.}

[207-207 title appD-elem-common-warnings default_required]
Common warnings

[210-216 p appD-elem-warn-surface-flux default_required]
Surface area and flux use different objects:
        \boxed{dS=|\mathbf r_u\times\mathbf r_v|\,du\,dv}
        is unsigned area.
        \boxed{d\mathbf S=(\mathbf r_u\times\mathbf r_v)\,du\,dv}
        is oriented vector area.

[218-221 p appD-elem-warn-surface-flux default_required]
Swapping the parameters u and v reverses orientation:
        \boxed{\mathbf r_v\times\mathbf r_u=-(\mathbf r_u\times\mathbf r_v).}

[223-225 p appD-elem-warn-surface-flux default_required]
Ordinary area and volume use positive scale factors.

[227-229 p appD-elem-warn-surface-flux default_required]
Differential forms keep orientation signs.
