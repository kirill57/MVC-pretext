[5-5 title sec-appA-parametric-and-polar-curves default_required]
Parametric and polar curves

[8-8 title appA-param-parametric-curves default_required]
Parametric curves

[10-20 p appA-param-parametric-curves default_required]
A plane curve can be written as
      \boxed{\mathbf r(t)=(x(t),y(t)).}
      A space curve can be written as
      \boxed{\mathbf r(t)=(x(t),y(t),z(t)).}
      The derivative is
      \boxed{\mathbf r'(t)=(x'(t),y'(t))}
      in the plane, and
      \boxed{\mathbf r'(t)=(x'(t),y'(t),z'(t))}
      in space.

[24-24 title appA-param-velocity-speed-and-acceleration default_required]
Velocity, speed, and acceleration

[26-30 p appA-param-velocity-speed-and-acceleration default_required]
\boxed{\mathbf v(t)=\mathbf r'(t)}
      \boxed{\text{speed}=|\mathbf r'(t)|}
      \boxed{\mathbf a(t)=\mathbf r''(t)}

[32-37 p appA-param-velocity-speed-and-acceleration default_required]
In the plane,
      \boxed{|\mathbf r'(t)|=\sqrt{(x'(t))^2+(y'(t))^2}.}
      In space,
      \boxed{|\mathbf r'(t)|=\sqrt{(x'(t))^2+(y'(t))^2+(z'(t))^2}.}

[41-41 title appA-param-arc-length default_required]
Arc length

[43-46 p appA-param-arc-length default_required]
For a curve \mathbf r(t), a\le t\le b,
      \boxed{L=\int_a^b |\mathbf r'(t)|\,dt.}

[50-50 title appA-param-slope-of-a-parametric-plane-curve default_required]
Slope of a parametric plane curve

[52-60 p appA-param-slope-of-a-parametric-plane-curve default_required]
If
      x'(t)\ne0,
      then
      \boxed{\frac{dy}{dx}=\frac{dy/dt}{dx/dt}.}
      If
      x'(t)=0\quad\text{and}\quad y'(t)\ne0,
      the tangent is vertical.

[64-64 title appA-param-common-parametrizations default_required]
Common parametrizations

[66-75 p appA-param-common-parametrizations default_required]
Line segment from P to Q:
      \boxed{\mathbf r(t)=P+t(Q-P),\qquad 0\le t\le1.}
      Circle of radius R, counterclockwise:
      \boxed{\mathbf r(t)=(R\cos t,R\sin t),\qquad 0\le t\le2\pi.}
      Circle of radius R, clockwise:
      \boxed{\mathbf r(t)=(R\cos t,-R\sin t),\qquad 0\le t\le2\pi.}
      Helix:
      \boxed{\mathbf r(t)=(R\cos t,R\sin t,ct).}

[79-79 title appA-param-polar-coordinates default_required]
Polar coordinates

[81-86 p appA-param-polar-coordinates default_required]
\boxed{x=r\cos\theta,\qquad y=r\sin\theta.}
      \boxed{r^2=x^2+y^2.}
      \boxed{\tan\theta=\frac{y}{x}}
      with quadrant care.

[88-93 p appA-param-polar-coordinates default_required]
A polar curve
      r=f(\theta)
      can be parametrized by
      \boxed{\mathbf r(\theta)=\bigl(f(\theta)\cos\theta,\ f(\theta)\sin\theta\bigr).}

[97-97 title appA-param-polar-area default_required]
Polar area

[99-104 p appA-param-polar-area default_required]
If r=f(\theta) traces the region once for
      \alpha\le\theta\le\beta,
      then
      \boxed{A=\frac12\int_\alpha^\beta f(\theta)^2\,d\theta.}

[108-108 title appA-param-polar-arc-length default_required]
Polar arc length

[110-113 p appA-param-polar-arc-length default_required]
For r=f(\theta),
      \boxed{L=\int_\alpha^\beta\sqrt{r^2+\left(\frac{dr}{d\theta}\right)^2}\,d\theta.}

[117-117 title appA-param-orientation-and-speed default_required]
Warning

[118-122 p appA-param-orientation-and-speed default_required]
A parametrization remembers orientation and speed. The same geometric curve may give
      different work integrals if it is traced in the opposite direction or traced multiple
      times.
