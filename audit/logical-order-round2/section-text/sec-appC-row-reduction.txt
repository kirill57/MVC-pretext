[5-5 title sec-appC-row-reduction default_required]
Row reduction

[8-8 title appC-row-linear-systems default_required]
Linear systems

[10-13 p appC-row-linear-systems default_required]
A system of linear equations can be written as
      \boxed{A\mathbf x=\mathbf b.}

[15-18 p appC-row-linear-systems default_required]
The augmented matrix is
      \boxed{[A\mid \mathbf b].}

[22-22 title appC-row-elementary-row-operations default_required]
Elementary row operations

[24-24 p appC-row-elementary-row-operations default_required]
The allowed row operations are:

[27-27 li appC-row-elementary-row-operations default_required]
Swap two rows.

[28-28 li appC-row-elementary-row-operations default_required]
Multiply a row by a nonzero scalar.

[29-29 li appC-row-elementary-row-operations default_required]
Add a multiple of one row to another row.

[32-32 p appC-row-elementary-row-operations default_required]
These operations do not change the solution set of the system.

[36-36 title appC-row-row-echelon-form default_required]
Row echelon form

[38-38 p appC-row-row-echelon-form default_required]
A matrix is in row echelon form if:

[41-41 li appC-row-row-echelon-form default_required]
All zero rows are at the bottom.

[42-42 li appC-row-row-echelon-form default_required]
Each leading entry is to the right of the leading entry above it.

[43-43 li appC-row-row-echelon-form default_required]
All entries below a leading entry are zero.

[48-48 title appC-row-reduced-row-echelon-form default_required]
Reduced row echelon form

[50-50 p appC-row-reduced-row-echelon-form default_required]
A matrix is in reduced row echelon form if it is in row echelon form and:

[53-53 li appC-row-reduced-row-echelon-form default_required]
Each leading entry is 1.

[54-54 li appC-row-reduced-row-echelon-form default_required]
Each leading 1 is the only nonzero entry in its column.

[59-59 title appC-row-pivot default_required]
Pivot

[61-61 p appC-row-pivot default_required]
A pivot is a leading nonzero entry in a row.

[63-63 p appC-row-pivot default_required]
A pivot column is a column containing a pivot.

[65-65 p appC-row-pivot default_required]
Variables corresponding to pivot columns are pivot variables.

[67-67 p appC-row-pivot default_required]
Variables corresponding to non-pivot columns are free variables.

[71-71 title appC-row-rank default_required]
Rank

[73-76 p appC-row-rank default_required]
The rank of a matrix is the number of pivots:
      \boxed{\operatorname{rank}(A)=\text{number of pivot columns in }A.}

[80-80 title appC-row-consistency-test default_required]
Consistency test

[82-87 p appC-row-consistency-test default_required]
For the system
      A\mathbf x=\mathbf b,
      row reduce
      [A\mid \mathbf b].

[89-95 p appC-row-consistency-test default_required]
If a row has the form
      \boxed{0\quad 0\quad \cdots\quad 0\mid c}
      with
      c\ne0,
      then the system has no solution.

[97-97 p appC-row-consistency-test default_required]
Otherwise, the system is consistent.

[101-101 title appC-row-number-of-solutions default_required]
Number of solutions

[103-103 p appC-row-number-of-solutions default_required]
For a consistent system:

[105-111 md appC-row-number-of-solutions default_required]
\begin{array}{c|c}
      \text{Situation} \amp \text{Solution type} \\ \hline
      \text{pivot in every variable column} \amp \text{unique solution}\\[1mm]
      \text{at least one free variable} \amp \text{infinitely many solutions}
      \end{array}

[115-115 title appC-row-homogeneous-systems default_required]
Homogeneous systems

[117-120 p appC-row-homogeneous-systems default_required]
A homogeneous system has the form
      \boxed{A\mathbf x=\mathbf 0.}

[122-126 p appC-row-homogeneous-systems default_required]
It is always consistent because
      \mathbf x=\mathbf 0
      is always a solution.

[128-128 p appC-row-homogeneous-systems default_required]
This is called the trivial solution.

[130-133 p appC-row-homogeneous-systems default_required]
A homogeneous system has a nontrivial solution exactly when there is at least one free
      variable.

[137-137 title appC-row-null-space default_required]
Null space

[139-142 p appC-row-null-space default_required]
The null space of A is
      \boxed{\operatorname{Null}(A)=\{\mathbf x:A\mathbf x=\mathbf 0\}.}

[144-144 p appC-row-null-space default_required]
It is the set of vectors that A sends to zero.

[148-148 title appC-row-column-space default_required]
Column space

[150-153 p appC-row-column-space default_required]
The column space of A is
      \boxed{\operatorname{Col}(A)=\text{span of the columns of }A.}

[155-160 p appC-row-column-space default_required]
The equation
      A\mathbf x=\mathbf b
      has a solution exactly when
      \boxed{\mathbf b\in\operatorname{Col}(A).}

[164-164 title appC-row-common-warnings default_required]
Common warnings

[165-168 p appC-row-common-warnings default_required]
Row operations preserve the solution set of a linear system, but they do not usually
      preserve determinants.

[170-175 p appC-row-common-warnings default_required]
Do not row reduce A alone if the system is
      A\mathbf x=\mathbf b.
      Row reduce the augmented matrix
      [A\mid \mathbf b].

[177-177 p appC-row-common-warnings default_required]
Free variables are not errors. They describe infinitely many solutions.
