Simulating flows and conservation laws

[8-8 title appG-flow-subsec-flow-lines default_required]
Flow lines

[10-15 p appG-flow-subsec-flow-lines default_required]
A flow line of a vector field
      \mathbf F
      solves the differential equation
      \frac{d\mathbf r}{dt}=\mathbf F(\mathbf r(t)).

[17-20 p appG-flow-subsec-flow-lines default_required]
In the plane:
      x'=P(x,y), \qquad y'=Q(x,y).

[22-25 p appG-flow-subsec-flow-lines default_required]
In space:
      x'=P(x,y,z), \qquad y'=Q(x,y,z), \qquad z'=R(x,y,z).

[29-29 title appG-flow-subsec-euler-method default_required]
Euler method

[31-36 p appG-flow-subsec-euler-method default_required]
For
      \mathbf r'=\mathbf F(\mathbf r),
      Euler's method is
      \mathbf r_{n+1} = \mathbf r_n+\Delta t\,\mathbf F(\mathbf r_n).

[38-40 p appG-flow-subsec-euler-method default_required]
It is simple but not very accurate.

[44-44 title appG-flow-subsec-euler-method-code default_required]
Euler method code

[46-56 program appG-flow-subsec-euler-method-code default_required]
def euler_flow(F, r0, dt, steps):
    r = np.array(r0, dtype=float)
    points = [r.copy()]

    for _ in range(steps):
        r = r + dt*np.array(F(r))
        points.append(r.copy())

    return np.array(points)

[122-122 title appG-flow-subsec-linearization-near-an-equilibrium default_required]
Linearization near an equilibrium

[124-129 p appG-flow-subsec-linearization-near-an-equilibrium default_required]
If
      \mathbf F(\mathbf p)=\mathbf 0,
      then near \mathbf p,
      \mathbf r' \approx J_{\mathbf F}(\mathbf p)(\mathbf r-\mathbf p).

[131-137 p appG-flow-subsec-linearization-near-an-equilibrium default_required]
The eigenvalues of
      J_{\mathbf F}(\mathbf p)
      describe the associated linear system. The table below records that linear behavior;
      applying it to a nonlinear field requires additional differential-equation results.
      In particular, purely imaginary eigenvalues do not determine nonlinear stability.

[172-176 p appG-flow-subsec-linearization-near-an-equilibrium default_required]
For example, the nonlinear field
      \mathbf F=(-y+x(x^2+y^2),x+y(x^2+y^2)) has eigenvalues \pm i
      at the origin. Nevertheless, along its nonconstant trajectories,
      \frac{d}{dt}(x^2+y^2)=2(x^2+y^2)^2\gt0, so they move outward rather

[180-180 title appG-flow-subsec-conservation-law default_required]
Conservation law

[182-185 p appG-flow-subsec-conservation-law default_required]
A conservation law has the form
      \frac{\partial \rho}{\partial t} + \nabla\cdot\mathbf J = s.

[187-189 p appG-flow-subsec-conservation-law default_required]
Here:

[192-192 title appG-flow-table-conservation-law-symbols default_required]
Symbols in the conservation law

[195-195 cell appG-flow-table-conservation-law-symbols default_required]
\rho

[196-196 cell appG-flow-table-conservation-law-symbols default_required]
density

[199-199 cell appG-flow-table-conservation-law-symbols default_required]
\mathbf J

[200-200 cell appG-flow-table-conservation-law-symbols default_required]
flux density

[203-203 cell appG-flow-table-conservation-law-symbols default_required]
s

[204-204 cell appG-flow-table-conservation-law-symbols default_required]
source rate

[209-212 p appG-flow-subsec-conservation-law default_required]
If there are no sources or sinks:
      \rho_t+\nabla\cdot\mathbf J=0.

[216-216 title appG-flow-subsec-integral-balance-law default_required]
Integral balance law

[218-221 p appG-flow-subsec-integral-balance-law default_required]
For a fixed region E,
      \frac{d}{dt}\iiint_E \rho\,dV = -\iint_{\partial E}\mathbf J\cdot\widehat{\mathbf n}\,dS + \iiint_E s\,dV.

[223-226 p appG-flow-subsec-integral-balance-law default_required]
In words:
      \text{rate of stored amount} = -\text{outward flux} + \text{source production}.

[230-230 title appG-flow-subsec-numerical-balance-check default_required]
Numerical balance check

[232-237 p appG-flow-subsec-numerical-balance-check default_required]
At two nearby times,
      \frac{A(t+\Delta t)-A(t)}{\Delta t}
      approximates
      \frac{dA}{dt}.

[239-242 p appG-flow-subsec-numerical-balance-check default_required]
Compare it to
      -\text{outward flux}+\text{source integral}.

[244-246 p appG-flow-subsec-numerical-balance-check default_required]
This is a useful diagnostic for simulations.

[250-250 title appG-flow-subsec-discrete-divergence-two-dimensions default_required]
Discrete divergence in two dimensions

[252-257 p appG-flow-subsec-discrete-divergence-two-dimensions default_required]
On a grid with spacing \Delta x,\Delta y, for
      \mathbf J=(M,N),
      a central-difference approximation is
      \nabla\cdot\mathbf J \approx \frac{M(x+\Delta x,y)-M(x-\Delta x,y)}{2\Delta x} + \frac{N(x,y+\Delta y)-N(x,y-\Delta y)}{2\Delta y}.

[261-261 title appG-flow-subsec-discrete-divergence-code default_required]
Discrete divergence code

[263-268 program appG-flow-subsec-discrete-divergence-code default_required]
def divergence2(M, N, dx, dy):
    M_y, M_x = np.gradient(M, dy, dx)
    N_y, N_x = np.gradient(N, dy, dx)
    return M_x + N_y

[270-273 p appG-flow-subsec-discrete-divergence-code default_required]
Array-axis warning:
      \texttt{np.gradient} \text{ usually returns } y\text{-derivatives first.}

[277-277 title appG-flow-subsec-advection-by-a-velocity-field default_required]
Advection by a velocity field

[279-284 p appG-flow-subsec-advection-by-a-velocity-field default_required]
If density moves with velocity
      \mathbf v,
      then the flux density is
      \mathbf J=\rho\mathbf v.

[286-289 p appG-flow-subsec-advection-by-a-velocity-field default_required]
With no sources:
      \rho_t+\nabla\cdot(\rho\mathbf v)=0.

[291-295 p appG-flow-subsec-advection-by-a-velocity-field default_required]
If
      \nabla\cdot\mathbf v=0
      and \rho is constant along moving particles, the flow is incompressible.

[310-312 p appG-flow-subsec-common-simulation-warnings default_required]

[314-316 p appG-flow-subsec-common-simulation-warnings default_required]
Boundary conditions matter.

[318-320 p appG-flow-subsec-common-simulation-warnings default_required]
