[73-73 title appA-deriv-rules default_required]
Derivative rules

[75-77 p appA-deriv-rules default_required]
\boxed{(f+g)'=f'+g'}

[79-81 p appA-deriv-rules default_required]
\boxed{(cf)'=cf'}

[83-85 p appA-deriv-rules default_required]
\boxed{(fg)'=f'g+fg'}

[87-89 p appA-deriv-rules default_required]
\boxed{\left(\frac{f}{g}\right)'=\frac{f'g-fg'}{g^2}\qquad (g\ne0)}

[91-94 p appA-deriv-rules default_required]
\boxed{\frac{d}{dx}f(g(x))=f'(g(x))g'(x)}
      This is the chain rule.

[98-98 title appA-deriv-linear-approximation default_required]
Linear approximation

[100-103 p appA-deriv-linear-approximation default_required]
If f is differentiable at a, then near a,
      \boxed{f(x)\approx f(a)+f'(a)(x-a).}

[105-108 p appA-deriv-linear-approximation default_required]
The tangent line is
      \boxed{L(x)=f(a)+f'(a)(x-a).}

[112-112 title appA-deriv-differential-notation default_required]
Differential notation

[114-116 p appA-deriv-differential-notation default_required]
\boxed{df=f'(x)\,dx.}

[118-122 p appA-deriv-differential-notation default_required]
The coordinate covector dx acts on an increment by dx(h)=h.
      For a small input change h, the predicted output change is
      df_x(h)=f'(x)h.

[124-129 p appA-deriv-differential-notation default_required]
Usually,
      \Delta f\ne df_x(h),
      but for small changes,
      \Delta f\approx df_x(h).

[178-178 title appA-deriv-warnings default_required]
Warnings

[180-183 p appA-deriv-warnings default_required]
Differentiability implies continuity:
      \boxed{f\text{ differentiable at }a \Longrightarrow f\text{ continuous at }a.}

[185-189 p appA-deriv-warnings default_required]
The converse is false. For example,
      f(x)=|x|
      is continuous at 0, but not differentiable at 0.
